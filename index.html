Earth Information Jukebox

Listen to Earth data from NASA images: convert image colors into values, then turn those values into sound.

Please note: The results are estimates based on image colors matched against NASA color bars, not raw satellite data. The ocean image comes from the ECCO2 model, not direct measurements. Forest data comes from the GEDI instrument aboard the International Space Station.
